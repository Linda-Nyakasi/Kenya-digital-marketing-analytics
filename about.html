About Us

How a small South B workshop became Nairobi Fashion Hub.

Nairobi Fashion Hub started in 2021 as a small tailoring outfit in South B, serving friends and family looking for well-fitted, affordable contemporary wear. Today we work with a network of local tailors and fabric suppliers across Nairobi to bring custom and ready-to-wear pieces to customers across Kenya, with same-day delivery available within the city. Every piece is inspected for quality before it leaves our workshop.

We keep our team small and our batches small, so fit issues get caught early rather than after delivery, and every order gets a level of attention that's hard to find at scale.
